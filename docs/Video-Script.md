# 3-minute video · script + shot list

**Format:** screen recording with your face in a corner (Loom / OBS / Google Meet recording). Speak at a normal pace. The script is ~420 words, which lands at about 2:50.

**B-roll:** [`demo-walkthrough.mp4`](demo-walkthrough.mp4) is a silent screen recording of the full product tour. Narrate over it, or record your own while following the shot list.

**Before recording:** open the site in an incognito window, set *Sim day* to 5, and keep these tabs ready: `/`, `/#/join`, `/#/whatsapp`, `/#/hq`, `/#/plan`.

---

### 0:00 – 0:15 · Hook
**Screen:** landing page, pixel campus animating.

> "NxtWave asked me to get 500 final-year students to register for a free AI workshop, with ₹2,000 and seven days. My first idea was a landing page plus Instagram ads. Five minutes of maths killed that idea, and that's where this started."

### 0:15 – 0:45 · The student
**Screen:** Growth Plan slide 1.

> "The target is final-year B.Tech students at Tier-2 and Tier-3 colleges in Telangana and AP, right in placement season. They care because interviewers ask 'tell me about your project', and now 'have you worked with AI?', and most of them don't have a real answer.
> The key insight: the bottleneck isn't awareness, it's trust. Students ignore brand webinars, but they open what a batchmate forwards in the class WhatsApp group."

### 0:45 – 1:20 · The plan
**Screen:** slide 2, then slide 3.

> "So the plan buys distribution through people. Three channels and one loop:
> One, thirty Quest Leaders posting tracked links in class WhatsApp groups.
> Two, ten clubs and placement cells co-hosting.
> Three, a squad referral loop with College Wars.
> The base case is 550, a 10% buffer. If we're under 150 by day 3, Plan B triggers. 75% of the ₹2,000 is paid only on results. And I designed for show-up, not just sign-ups."

### 1:20 – 2:30 · What I built (demo)
**Screen:** follow the clicks.

1. **Landing:** "Every student walking into the AI Lab is a registration."
2. **Join → Quest Pass:** "Registering takes 30 seconds. You get a Quest Pass for WhatsApp Status with a QR to your invite link, and the message is in Tenglish and Hinglish, because that's how students actually text." Click **+ Friend joins** ×3: "Loot unlocks as friends join."
3. **Leader kit:** "Each Quest Leader gets a tracked link with an A/B message hook, ready-to-post messages and a printable QR poster."
4. **WhatsApp:** tap *Count me in*. "Students can register without leaving WhatsApp. Then four reminders protect show-up, and the whole flow costs about ₹280 at Meta's rate."
5. **Command Center:** press **▶ Play 7 days**. "This is what I'd run the campaign from: sign-ups against the plan, channel pace, the A/B test (the placement hook won with 94% confidence), stalled leaders with a one-tap nudge, and the forecast."

### 2:30 – 3:00 · How I thought
**Screen:** AI Notes page.

> "AI suggested an iPad lucky draw and 'only 50 seats left'. I rejected both: they attract the wrong people and burn the trust the plan runs on.
> With 24 more hours, I'd test both message hooks on 20 real final-years before day 1, and wire up the real WhatsApp bot.
> Idea, build, launch, measure, learn, scale: that's exactly how I'd like to work on your team. Thank you."

### Recording tips
- Do one practice run, then record in a single take. Small stumbles are fine; this is about thinking, not polish.
- Zoom the browser to 110% so text is readable in the video.
- Keep the face cam small (bottom-right) so the dashboard stays visible.
